# Can I use the programme on my phone and laptop?

Your programme works on one device. Choose the one you will practise with. You can move it later — see [Using the Academy on your device](your-device.md).

# Can I download the videos?

No. Videos are streamed so the programme stays protected. Worksheets and charts can be downloaded from each lesson and from **Downloads**.

# How long do I have access?

Usually one year from your start date. Your exact date is on your dashboard.

# Can I share my login with family?

Please do not. Each programme is personal, works on one device, and your name appears on the videos.

# I feel unwell during a practice. What should I do?

Stop the practice, rest, and follow the health and safety note you accepted at the start. If you take medicine for diabetes, check your blood sugar if you feel dizzy, shaky or sweaty. Contact your doctor or our team if you are unsure. In an emergency, call emergency services.

# Will my progress be lost if I change phone?

No. Progress, notes and access are saved to your account, not your phone.

# Is my information private?

Yes. Only our team can see your progress, to support you. Your notes on lessons are visible only to you.
