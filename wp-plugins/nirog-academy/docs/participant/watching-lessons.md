# In short

Open **Today** and press the big button — it takes you to your next lesson, or back to where you stopped. Videos and audio remember your place. Booklets open right on the course pages and remember the page. Everything you have opened is also in your **Library**.

# Videos and audio

1. Press play. On a phone the video is at the top of the page; turn your phone sideways or press full screen for a bigger picture.
2. Under the video: **↺ 10s** goes back 10 seconds, **10s ↻** goes forward, and **1×** changes the speed (1.25×, 1.5×, 0.75×).
3. Read the **Before you start** box if there is one — it tells you who should skip or change a practice.
4. The lesson shows **Completed** when you have watched most of it (about 90%). Skipping ahead does not count.
5. Audio practices (Yoga Nidra, pranayama) keep playing when you lock your phone.

Some lessons are videos from the Nirog Bhumi YouTube channel. They play in the same place and also remember where you stopped.

Quality adjusts to your internet speed. On slow mobile data the picture may look softer — that is normal.

# Booklets

1. Open the booklet from its week, from **Today**, or from **Library → Booklets**, then tap the book or **Start reading**. The booklet opens over the whole screen.
2. Choose how to read with the buttons at the top:
   - **Scroll** (the usual choice on a phone): pages one under another — just scroll down. Use **−** and **+** at the bottom to make the text larger.
   - **Book** (the usual choice on a computer or tablet): pages turn like a real book. Tap the edge of the page, swipe, or use the arrow keys.
3. **Pages** jumps to any page. **← Close** (or your phone's back button) takes you back.
4. The booklet is done when you have read it to the end. Next time, it opens on the page where you stopped.

Some booklets have a **Download** button; others can only be read on the course pages.

# Your Library

- **Booklets** — every weekly booklet, with "Read", "Continue reading" or the date it opens.
- **Bonus** — extra talks, recipes and recordings, grouped by topic.
- **Downloads** — charts and worksheets from your lessons.

# Notes

Every lesson has **My notes**. Only you can see them. They save by themselves.

# If this doesn't work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Opens on …" | Lessons open day by day. Come back on that date. |
| "Finish the previous lesson first" | This programme goes in order. |
| "Playback stopped because this account started playing somewhere else" | Your account plays one video at a time. Close the other tab. |
| "Reload to continue watching" | The page was open a long time. Reload it. |
| The booklet does not open | Reload the page. Make sure your browser is up to date. |
