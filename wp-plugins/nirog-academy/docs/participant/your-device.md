# In short

Your programme works on **one device** — the first phone or computer you log in from. This keeps your programme private to you. If you change phone, you can move your programme yourself with a code we email you, once every 30 days. After that, our team can do it for you.

# Why one device?

Programme lessons are made for you, as part of your treatment plan. The one-device rule stops accounts from being shared, so we can keep the programme affordable and personal.

# What counts as a "new device"

The website can only recognise your **browser**, not the phone itself. So Nirog Bhumi Courses will see a new device if you:

- use a different phone, tablet or computer,
- switch browser on the same phone (for example Chrome to Samsung Internet),
- use a private or incognito window,
- clear your browser's data or cookies.

**Tip:** use one browser, in normal mode, and avoid "clear browsing data" for this website.

# Move your programme to this device

1. Log in on the new device. You will see **Your programme is on another device**.
2. Press **Email me a code**.
3. Open the email and type the 6-digit code. It works for 15 minutes.
4. Press **Confirm this device**. Your old device is signed out straight away.

# If you changed device recently

If you already moved your programme in the last 30 days, you will see **Ask our team to move your programme**. Write a short note (for example "my phone was replaced") and press **Send request**. We usually reply within one working day.

# If this doesn't work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "This code has expired" | Press **Send a new code** and use the newest email. |
| "That code is not right" | Check you typed all 6 digits from the most recent email. |
| "Too many codes requested" | Wait an hour, or send a request to our team. |
| The email about a device change was not you | Contact us straight away and change your password. |

# Words used here

- **Registered device**: the browser on the phone or computer your programme works on.
- **Device change code**: a 6-digit number we email you to confirm a new device.
