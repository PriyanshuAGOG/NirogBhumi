# In short

After you join a programme, we email you a link to choose your password. Use it on the phone or computer you will practise with, because that becomes your registered device. Then press **Start the lesson** on your **Today** page.

# Steps

1. Open the email titled **Welcome to …** from Nirog Bhumi.
2. Press **Choose your password**. The button works for 24 hours.
3. Type a password of at least 8 characters, twice, and press **Save and continue**.
4. You are now on your **Today** page. Press **Start the lesson**.
5. The first time, you will see a short **health and safety note**. Read it, tick the box and continue.

# Logging in later

- Go to Nirog Bhumi Courses and enter your email and password.
- Or choose **Email me a login link** and use the link we send (it works once, for 15 minutes).

# If this doesn't work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No welcome email | Check your Spam or Promotions folder. Search your inbox for "Nirog Bhumi". |
| "This link has expired" | Press **Send me a new link** and use the newest email. |
| "That email and password do not match" | Use **Forgot password?** or **Email me a login link**. |
| "Too many attempts" | Wait 15 minutes, then try again or use a login link. |

# Words used here

- **Dashboard**: the home page of Nirog Bhumi Courses, showing your programme and progress.
- **Registered device**: the one phone or computer your programme works on. See [Using Nirog Bhumi Courses on your device](your-device.md).
