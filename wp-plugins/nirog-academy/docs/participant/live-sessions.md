# In short

Live sessions are listed under **Live**. The **Join now** button appears 15 minutes before the start. Recordings of past sessions are linked under **Past sessions** when they are ready.

# Steps

1. Open **Live** from the menu.
2. Press **Add to Google Calendar** so you do not forget.
3. On the day, open **Live** again. From 15 minutes before the start, press **Join now**.

We remind you the day before and shortly before the session (if reminders are on in Profile).

# If this doesn't work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No Join button | It appears 15 minutes before the start. Reload the page. |
| The meeting app asks you to install something | Follow its steps, or open the link in your browser. |
