# In short

Each live session has its own page in the Academy. Open it from **Live**, from your week, or from the reminder email. From 15 minutes before the start, the page shows **Join the session** (it opens Zoom or Google Meet) — or the class plays right on the page.

# Steps

1. Open **Live** and choose the session. Press **Add to calendar** so you do not forget.
2. On the day, open the session page again (the reminder email has a button).
3. From 15 minutes before the start:
   - **Join the session** opens the meeting app, or
   - the class plays on the page — just press play.
4. Keep your microphone off unless you are asked to speak.

We remind you the day before and shortly before the start (if reminders are on in **Account**).

# Afterwards

When the recording is ready, the session page shows **Watch the recording**.

# If this doesn't work

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No Join button | It appears 15 minutes before the start. Reload the page. |
| "Cancelled" | The session will not take place; we will tell you the new time. |
| The meeting app asks you to install something | Follow its steps, or open the link in your browser. |
| The class does not play | Reload the page at the start time. Check your internet. |
