# In short

Most problems are solved by reloading the page, checking your internet connection, or using an up-to-date browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox). If you are told your programme is on another device, see [Using Nirog Bhumi Courses on your device](your-device.md).

# The video does not play

1. Reload the page.
2. Check your internet. On mobile data, move closer to a window or switch to Wi-Fi.
3. Update your browser. Very old browsers cannot play protected videos.
4. Close other tabs that are playing a lesson.
5. If you see "Reload to continue watching", reload — the page was open a long time.

# I cannot log in

- Use **Email me a login link** on the login page.
- Or use **Forgot password?** to choose a new password.
- If you see **Your programme is on another device**, follow the steps on that screen.

# I do not receive emails

- Check Spam, Promotions and Updates folders.
- Search for "Nirog Bhumi".
- Make sure you are checking the email address you used when you joined.

# A lesson is locked

- "Opens on …" means it opens on that date.
- "Complete … first" means the programme goes in order.
- "Access ended" or "Access paused" — contact us.

# Still stuck?

Write to us from the **Help** page. Tell us what you were trying to do, what you saw, and (if you can) attach a screenshot.
